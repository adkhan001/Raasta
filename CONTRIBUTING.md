# Contributing to Raasta

Please open an issue describing the problem or proposed experiment before a substantial change.

For bugs, include your Colab runtime, notebook cell, profile, relevant settings and complete error text. Remove credentials and personal paths. A small reproducible example is helpful.

For model improvements, report the training profile, seed, checkpoint selection rule and held-out per-class metrics. Keep the test set separate from tuning. Do not report confidence as accuracy or claim Pakistan validation without local evidence.

Keep the notebook usable entirely in Colab. Preserve Drive checkpoints and session restoration. Do not commit datasets or trained weights. Credit upstream datasets and methods.
